:::note[The whole mod in three sentences]
**XP is gone.** You earn **Skill Shards** from advancements instead, and spend them on a 15-lane skill tree, at the anvil, and on enchanting. **Five craftable gear tiers** slot in between the vanilla ones, and **crates**, **quests** and a **shop** round it out.
:::

Runs on **Fabric** and **NeoForge** (Minecraft **26.2** / **26.1.2**). Install the jar on the **server** (Fabric needs Fabric API) — players install nothing and get the textures pushed to them on join. Or use the ready-made [VSP Player/Server packs](/modhub/mods/vanillaskills/#playing-via-the-vsp-modpack).

## Where's my XP?

Removed — nothing drops or grants it. The XP bar shows your banked **Skill Shards** instead. Servers can put vanilla XP back with one config switch (`experienceEnabled`).

## How do I earn shards?

Do **advancements** — that's the main source: tasks pay **5**, goals **20**, purple challenges **50**. Also: mine **shard ore**, fish up **crates**, sell to the **wandering trader**, and loot structure chests. And just play: mining, building and harvesting all have a small chance to shake a shard loose every once in a while — luck (the Fortune Finder lane) improves those odds.

**Quest Shards** are a second currency: earn them from **Bounty Board** quests (`/quests`), spend them on the gear-unlock lanes and the Quest Shop. 3 Quest Shards convert to 1 Skill Shard, one way only.

## How do I spend shards?

Open the tree with **`/skill`**. Fifteen lanes — health, speed, mining, luck, damage, armor, reach, swimming, gear unlocks and more. Every node is a live modifier, and you can respec any time. Shards can also be **withdrawn as items**, traded, and banked again with a right-click.

## How do I enchant?

The enchanting table is now the **Infusing Table** — no lapis, no levels. Put enchanted books in **chiseled bookshelves** around it: it offers exactly what's shelved, charges shards, and **never consumes the books**. Shelve a book once, enchant with it forever.

Anvils no longer put books on gear — that's the Infusing Table's job. Anvils still combine two books.

## What does the anvil cost?

Shards, not levels: **1 per repair material**, **1 per enchant level** on the item you sacrifice. Renames are **free**. Vanilla's prior-work penalty no longer compounds, so nothing becomes "too expensive."

## What's the gear ladder?

**Hardwood → Rose Gold → Steel → Crystalline → Dragon**, woven in between the vanilla tiers — Steel sits between iron and diamond, Crystalline between diamond and netherite, Dragon above netherite. Each set has a **full-set bonus** (checked live), and crafting each tier is unlocked through the **Armorsmith** / **Toolsmith** lanes.

- **Steel** is smelted, not crafted: one **iron block** → 3 Steel Ingots in any furnace.
- Full stats for every tier: [Gear & crafting](/modhub/mods/vanillaskills/gear/).

## How do I get Dragon gear?

<Steps>

1. Kill the **Ender Dragon yourself** — 8 **Dragon Scales** per kill, 32 for the world's first.

2. Forge a **Dragon Ingot**: Netherite Ingot + 4 scales.

3. Upgrade netherite gear at a **smithing table** with the ingot and a Dragon Template.

4. Repair: **one scale + 2 shards** fully fixes a piece. Bonus: drop a Dragon chestplate and an Elytra on an anvil and they **fuse**.

</Steps>

## Why can't I mine this?

- **Deepslate** needs a **Steel-tier-or-better** pickaxe (vanilla diamond/netherite count).
- **Shard ore** (reinforced deepslate) needs **Netherite, Crystalline or Dragon** — anything weaker shatters it and you get nothing.

## What are the crates?

**Fished out of the water**, right-click to open (it spins a little slot reel). Wooden/Copper/Iron/Diamond as a rarity ladder, plus Frozen, Lush and Desert biome crates. **Luck of the Sea** catches you a few more crates; the **Unboxing** rod enchantment upgrades which crate bites.

## Portable storage?

**Chest + 8 leather = Satchel** — a shulker box in all but name, no End trip required.

## Running a server?

- Config lives at `<world>/vanillaskills/gameplay.json` — every stat, cost and toggle. Live-reload with `/skill reload`.
- The skill tree, quests, shop and crates are **datapacks** — reprice, replace or remove anything like vanilla tags.
- Useful commands: `/skill skillshards <player> add <n>`, `/skill give <item>`, `/quests board`.
